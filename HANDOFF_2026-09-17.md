## Gotchas that cost real time — do not relearn these

1. **A texture that appears black is usually an R3F material-reuse bug.** R3F
   reconciles `mesh` + `meshBasicMaterial` by element type and reuses the
   instance, so giving a material a `map` *after* it was compiled without one
   leaves the sampler unbuilt and the texture renders black. Fix: the mapped
   mesh gets its own `key`, so it mounts fresh. This is amendment A37 and it is
   why AJ's photos "were not showing".
2. **Zoom is the field of view, not the camera distance.** Moving the rig in
   re-composes the shot and pushes the room's content off the top of the frame.
3. **A camera behind the player cannot see over a 3-tall wall unless its pitch
   exceeds ~57°.** At the original `(0, 10, 8)` the player vanished completely
   behind the south wall for every tile south of `z≈10.7`; it went unnoticed
   only because everyone spawns at `x=9`, where the doorway gap is.
4. **`useFrame` clamps `dt` to 0.05s**, so under software GL the world clock
   runs at roughly 0.6× wall time. The belt's 12.2s cycle takes ~17-20s. Probes
   must poll for an observable signal (a card's photo being fetched) instead of
   sleeping for a nominal duration.
5. **A cold `.next` needs `fonts.gstatic.com`.** On a cold dev cache, a font
   fetch timeout makes *every* route answer 500 (once, misleadingly, 404) until
   the font lands in `.next/dev/static/media`. Not a code bug.
6. **The Windows sandbox helper is flaky on this machine.** Non-approved
   commands fail with `helper_unknown_error: apply deny-read ACLs`; only
   pre-approved prefixes or escalated commands get through, and `view_image`
   fails the same way (restarting Codex fixes it). When it is working, the
   screenshots and probes are the fastest way to understand what the world
   actually looks like.
7. **Wall frames hang on side walls.** A camera fixed to the south views them
   at a grazing angle from anywhere in the room — that, not distance, was the
   real cause of "I can barely see the writings on the wall". The uncommitted
   camera turn exists to fix it.
